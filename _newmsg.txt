fix(protocol): hardware-verified BootROM entry, firmware base and read framing

All of the following was measured on a real M-241 (V1.13 / 01017-00F /
MT3318, Windows 11, CP210x on COM3) with read-only commands.

BootROM entry:
  The logger accepts $PMTK180*3B\r\n at the NMEA baud and then listens at
  115200. DTR and RTS must be asserted and held, and the a0 0a 50 05 probe
  sequence must be sent back to back: inserting pauses makes the handshake
  time out. The reply to each probe is accepted either as the probe byte or
  as its bitwise complement.

Firmware location and read framing:
  - the image lives at 0x00200000, not 0x20000000 (0x20000000 reads
    '42 20 02 02' = unmapped bus)
  - READ32's count field is in 16-bit words, not bytes
  - READ32 replies are echo(9) + data with NO checksum trailer
  - each 32-bit word arrives byte-reversed

Toolkit changes:
  - BASE_ADDR_FIRMWARE = 0x00200000 (+ BASE_ADDR_FIRMWARE_MT333X for probing)
  - PMTK180_COMMAND / DEFAULT_NMEA_BAUD constants; _send_pmtk180 now sends
    the plain sentence and switches baud back afterwards instead of
    appending ',<baud>'
  - _assert_lines holds DTR/RTS instead of pulsing them
  - _sync_stage accepts probe or ~probe
  - simulator maps both bases so existing tests keep working

Real-hardware results recorded in docs/10-hardware-session-notes.md:
  two independent 406,620-byte dumps, identical sha256 ee52d339..., correct
  '18 f0 9f e5' vector table, 14 documented runtime-field deltas versus the
  official package, and a patched image whose only 3 changed bytes are the
  documented ones.
